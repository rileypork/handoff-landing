How a haul gets booked

Handoff is the LA Marketplace delivery API for agents. Sellers list, agents book the haul, Westside stays the focus.

  1. 01

    You list as usual

    Sellers post on Marketplace the way they already do. Handoff does not become the storefront.

  2. 02

    An agent requests a quote

    A partner or agent asks Handoff for a quote through the API. Pricing does not live on this page.

  3. 03

    A courier moves it

    Pickup and drop-off stay on the Westside. Humans still haul while supply stays small.

Small and hatch-sized first: nightstands, chairs, bikes, and other stuff that fits a hatchback.
